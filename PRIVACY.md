# Kwitch Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Kwitch is a browser extension that shows your live followed Twitch and Kick channels together in the sidebar of both sites.

## Summary

Kwitch does not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. The developer receives nothing from the extension. There are no analytics, no tracking and no ads.

## What the extension accesses

**Login cookies for Twitch and Kick.** Kwitch reads your existing Twitch login cookie (`auth-token`) and Kick login cookie (`session_token`) from your browser. They are used only to ask Twitch and Kick for your own list of live followed channels. The cookie values are sent only to twitch.tv and kick.com, the sites they belong to. They are not saved by the extension and are not sent anywhere else.

**Your live followed channels.** The list of channels you follow that are currently live (name, avatar, category, title and viewer count) is fetched from Twitch and Kick and shown in the sidebar. It is kept in memory for a short time to avoid repeated requests and is never sent to the developer or any third party.

**Your settings.** Your chosen main viewing site and any streamer name links you add are saved in your browser's extension storage. If browser sync is enabled, your browser may sync these settings between your own devices.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not send any data to the developer or to third parties.
- It does not read or change anything on websites other than twitch.tv and kick.com.
- It does not track your browsing or viewing activity.
- It does not load or run code from a remote server.

## Third parties

Kwitch communicates only with Twitch (twitch.tv) and Kick (kick.com), using your existing login. Your use of those services is covered by their own privacy policies.

Kwitch is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Twitch or Kick.
